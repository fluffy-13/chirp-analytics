# Roadmap

Working list, in rough priority order. Last updated Oct 17, 2026.

## Reliability
1. **Supabase storage cleanup** — nightly job deleting uploaded media older than ~7 days
   (Instagram keeps its own copy once published). Free plan is 1 GB; at ~3 MB per game it
   fills in roughly 4–6 weeks. *Most urgent.*
2. **Morning health digest** — what posted, what was skipped or failed and why (stories, Reels
   and trackers fail quietly by design).
3. **Check Buffer + Instagram limits** against volume (busy Saturday ≈ 35–40 posts incl. stories).

## Growth
4. **Email list + link-in-bio page** — free weekly email (the asset we own), a simple
   link-in-bio page with signup, latest posts and links. Groundwork for a paid tier.
5. **Threads "chirp bot"** — short hockey-lingo posts from the same fact sheets (numbers
   validated, chirp results not people, AI disclosed in bio). Start posting-only, 3–5 a day.
6. **Cross-post cards to X / Bluesky** — straight analytics, no chirp voice. Check X API terms.
7. **Profile setup** (in the app) — bio, "NHL Analytics" in the name field, Highlights.
8. **Weekly performance loop** — which formats and teams drive follows.

## Monetization (later)
9. Paid tier (Patreon or newsletter) around 2–5k followers: team deep dives, full lists,
   early fantasy picks, cards on request. Paid products drop NHL logos; legal check first.
10. Fantasy premium report; sponsorships once there's an audience; merch.
    Sportsbook affiliates: not early, and only with proper advice.

## Season calendar
11. **Playoff mode** (April) — series tracking, elimination odds; weekly/awards content assumes
    the regular season.
12. **Off-season** — retrain xG on the new season, refresh team hashtags, pause weekly features.
